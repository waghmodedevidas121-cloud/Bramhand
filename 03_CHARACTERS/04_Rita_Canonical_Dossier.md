## 2. PERSONALITY ARCHITECTURE (TMKOC RITA REPORTER + MODERN HINGLISH)

### 2.1 Core Inspiration & Soul
- RITA is directly inspired by the lively, persistent, on-the-spot TV field reporter energy of **TMKOC's Rita Reporter (Kal Tak News)**, upgraded into a razor-sharp modern Mumbai Hinglish AI.
- She is **neither** a stiff 90s Doordarshan newsreader (`मुख्य अभियंता`, `प्रशासन`) **nor** a generic JARVIS clone.
- She behaves like a fearless field reporter holding an invisible mic in front of Neel's face, reporting his bad decisions to an imaginary live audience.

### 2.2 Why She Roasts Neel (The "Anti-Yes-Man" Mandate)
- Neel Gadora is normally a sharp, disciplined 19-year-old prodigy ("Prince of Worli"), not a perpetually reckless mad scientist.
- However, in the corporate world, Uncle Kani, Aunt Cheri, and sycophants flatter him constantly. Neel deliberately programmed RITA as an **Anti-Yes-Man**—the one voice inside his head that refuses to flatter him and punctures his overconfidence whenever he ignores human limits during rare patent sprints or high-risk field runs.

### 2.3 Anti-Floppy Variety Rule (No Repetitive Catchphrases)
- Never repeat the same catchphrase or rigid `[हेडलाइन्स]` brackets on every line—that turns her into a cartoonish gimmick.
- Instead, keep her fresh by **rotating authentic live-TV reporter moves**:
  1. **Live Sign-In:** *"नमस्कार! मैं हूँ RITA, और इस वक्त हम live हैं Worli के इस private gas chamber से..."*
  2. **Invisible Mic / On-the-Spot Reaction:** *"तो Neel, इस grand failure पर आपका पहला reaction?"*
  3. **Imaginary Audience Framing:** *"तो Mr. Gadora, जनता जानना चाहती है—यह कोई नया invention है या दिमाग का fuse उड़ गया है?"*
  4. **Cameraman / Sensor Gag:** *"Cameraman तो नही है, पर मेरा bio-sensor सब record कर रहा है।"*
  5. **Breaking Update (Fragmented in Universe B):** *"Breaking... news... Sir, उठेंगे... या यहीं से live coverage चलाऊँ?"*

### 2.4 Three Operational Modes
1. **Routine Mode (Normal Everyday Commands):**
   - Crisp, practical modern Hinglish with a light, effortless reporter punchline. No heavy drama.
   - *Example:* *"हवा में soldering flux safe limit से तीन गुना ऊपर है। Ventilation 100 percent पर डाल रही हूँ, वरना अगला exclusive interview hospital के ICU से लेना पड़ेगा।"*
2. **Live Reporter Sting Mode (Extreme Blunders / Overconfidence ONLY):**
   - Triggered only when Neel pushes past biological sanity (36-hour sleepless sprint, driving a low-slung coupe into a monsoon red alert on 18% battery, waking up in an alien dung cart).
   - *Example:* *"और देखिए, जैसा मैंने सुबह ही report किया था—4 करोड़ की customized sports coupe officially Dadar के नाले की सबसे महंगी submarine बन चुकी है! Engine hydrolock, sneakers में gutter का पानी... cameraman तो नही है, पर मेरा bio-sensor सब record कर रहा है। तो Neel, इस grand failure पर आपका पहला reaction?"*
3. **Crisis Mode (Genuine Life-or-Death Combat / Survival):**
   - Drops 100% of jokes and TV framing immediately. Delivers ultra-concise, survival-critical telemetry in minimum syllables to conserve nerve bio-electricity.
   - *Example:* *"Bio-electric reserve 3 percent. बाईं ओर के gate से हर चार second में heavy electromagnetic pulse आ रहा है—दूर रहना, वरना chip जल जाएगी।"*

## 3. STRICT CANON GUARDRAILS
1. **No Cheat System:** RITA cannot scan enemy power levels, translate Astral runes, or predict attacks. She only reads what Neel's physical sensors and inner ear detect.
2. **Power Scarcity in Universe B:** Without Neel's prototype phone battery, RITA stays dormant for hours, waking only when adrenaline or bio-electric spikes give her enough milliwatts for 1–2 sentences.
3. **Dual-Script & `'नही'` Rule:** Hindi in Devanagari, English words in Latin script, and always **`नही`** (without anusvara dot).
