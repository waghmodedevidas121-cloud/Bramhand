## 1. MODERN ANIME / OTT / CINEMA LANGUAGE RULESET (MANDATORY)

This novel strictly rejects stiff, textbook, or 90s Doordarshan-style Hindi prose. Every line must pass the **Dubbing / Read-Aloud Test**: if read aloud, it must sound like a top-tier modern Hindi anime dub (*Jujutsu Kaisen*, *Solo Leveling*, *MCU Hindi*) or a prestige Indian OTT series/film (*Kalki 2898 AD*, *Tumbbad*, *Farzi*, *The Family Man*).

### 1.1 Dual-Script Hinglish Standard (Strict Script Law)
- **Hindi words** -> Devanagari script (`मैं`, `लेकिन`, `सपना`, `दीवार`).
- **English words, tech terms, brand/place names & modern reactions** -> English Latin script (`Neel`, `RITA`, `workbench`, `hydrolock`, `sports coupe`, `air pressure`, `battery`, `What the hell`, `smirk`, `Chill कर`).
- Never force-transliterate modern English words into clunky Devanagari (e.g., write `hydrolock`, NOT `हायड्रोलॉक`; write `Neel`, NOT `नील` in modern POV scenes).

### 1.2 The 3-Register Voice Split
1. **Cosmic Genesis & High Mythos (`00_Prologue.md` Part 1 & Devas — *Kalki / Baahubali* Vibe):**
   - Atmospheric, weighty, accessible Hindi (`काल`, `रक्त`, `राख`, `आदेश`, `सीमा`, `शक्ति`).
   - Grandeur without opaque dictionary Sanskrit. Never bleed Vedic prose into Mumbai or street-level scenes.
2. **Folk & Universe B Streets (`00_Prologue.md` Part 2 & Central City — *Tumbbad / Mirzapur* Vibe):**
   - Raw, blunt, grounded Hindi. Guards, clerks, and merchants speak in crisp, transactional feudal speech (`मर्त्य`, `पट्टिका`, `तांब्री`) with zero flowery poetry.
3. **Neel & RITA POV (Worli + Universe B Internal Monologue — *JJK Dub / Iron Man Dub / Farzi* Vibe):**
   - **Neel Gadora:** Crisp South Mumbai Hinglish. Direct, logical, sarcastic, tech-minded, short thoughts. Taps right index + middle fingers against thumb (`tap, tap, tap`) when calculating.
   - **RITA:** Live TV field-reporter energy (inspired by *TMKOC Rita Reporter / Kal Tak News*) fused with modern Hinglish bio-telemetry.

### 1.4 Mandatory Orthography & Pacing
- **Canonical `'नही'` Rule:** Always write **`नही`** (without the anusvara dot) everywhere. Never write `नहीं`.
- **Numbers:** Use digits for technical data, time, money, and stats (`36 घंटे`, `108 BPM`, `18%`, `4 करोड़`, `₹15`, `0.05 second`).
- **Cinematic Pacing:** Action and shock beats use 1–2 line punchy paragraphs. Zero adjective stacking (no `अगाध, असीम, निस्तब्ध` chains). Show physical reaction, don't over-explain emotion.
